# Staff allergen declarations

- Staff can manually enter ingredients, contains and may contain text for an existing product.
- Barcode lookup returns a review draft from the matched product only. Missing data stays blank and does not imply absence.
- Require a verification source and explicit confirmation before display. Staff can hide a stale declaration and retain the text for review.
- Scope product reads and changes to the current vendor. Show verified declarations on product details, and preserve the general contact notice.
- Use staging fixtures. Do not backfill or verify real product declarations in this slice.
