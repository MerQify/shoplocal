# Requirements

- **R1** Product favourites render the same heart silhouette in signed-out, unsaved and saved states, with a fill only when saved and with the existing accessible names, pressed state and touch target.
- **R2** The floating basket trigger and drawer heading render a 20 px functional basket glyph, with a visible Basket label on the trigger from the small breakpoint and the existing count, subtotal, drawer behaviour and empty-state medallion intact.
- **R3** Product and basket quantity controls render matching 16 px plus and minus glyphs and preserve their accessible names and hit areas.
- **R4** Shopping-list review, checkout slot selection and the checkout payment reassurance use functional unavailable-item, timed-slot and shield-check glyphs respectively.
- **R5** Compact list-review and checkout order-result messages use distinct functional alert, pending and confirmation glyphs beside the existing text, including distinct pending, confirmed and cancelled outcomes on the order-result page.
- **R6** The changes use the existing semantic registry and vendor tokens and pass the generated-icon, lint, typecheck and test gates.
